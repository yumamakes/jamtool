Demons Chord Blocks

Four pages, one chord each. Play only the three notes of that page's chord, and stay locked to the band. Hit Play: you get 4 count-in clicks, then the band comes in on beat 1. The yellow marker shows exactly where you are in the bar.

Read the gridOne box = one bar of 4 beats, split into 16 slots: 1 e & a 2 e & a … A colored block starts where you play and stretches as long as you hold it.
Note lengthsWhole = 4 beats (16 slots) · Half = 2 beats (8) · Quarter = 1 beat (4) · Eighth = half a beat (2). Dashed box = rest. Stay silent.
The rule for every levelClap it with the track first. Then play it. Tick the box after 3 clean loops in a row without speeding up.

The band plays in 4-bar phrases. A crash cymbal marks beat 1 of every phrase, and a snare roll on beat 4 of bar 4 warns that a new phrase is coming. Pop Rock is the easiest groove for keeping time. Demons is the song's real beat, used for the kick-drum levels. Once all four pages are ticked off, it's time to put I–V–vi–IV together.
